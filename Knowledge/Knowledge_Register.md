## Capture and revise

Record a short title, stable knowledge ID, related project IDs, finding, conditions, source and version or date, evidence state, and what someone can do differently. For a failure, capture the observable miss, consequence, attempted fix, and any retest. For progress, record which claim the observed output supports and which prerequisite or question remains.

Use these evidence states as needed: **lead**, **documented**, **observed in a named run**, or **reused with evidence**. State open questions beside the findings they qualify. When new evidence contradicts an entry or changes its scope, link the revision to the earlier entry so both the lesson and its history remain recoverable.
