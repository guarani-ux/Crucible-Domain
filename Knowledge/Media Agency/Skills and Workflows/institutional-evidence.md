# Calgary Public Library institutional practice evidence

Public website, public governance records, and a purposive read-only sample of employer task and comment records. November 16, 2026. Designed as evidence input for the Work Operating System and Media Agency practice design.

## How to read the evidence

Public sources show institutional commitments, published service paths, and reported outcomes. Task hierarchy, handoff notes, and comments show how particular workers organized and discussed assignments. Interpret a due date, draft link, favorable comment, explicit approval, reported send, publication and actual audience receipt as different observations. Named authority is project-specific. The practice actions and acceptance checks below derive from the observations and remain proposed design, except where explicitly reported as observed.

The direct Asana sample was selected for contrasting decisions from the November 15 Work Operating System Practice Inquiry, from related project rosters and nested tasks, and from targeted channel and production searches. The coverage receipt at the end records the confirmed record counts. This is a synthesis of public plans and internal work practice. The links below point to public sources. Authorized workers can recover source task pointers from *What the Work Operating System needs to carry*, Case sources, and locate the additional named work families in their authorized Asana workspace.

Direct child reads distinguish first cut, Production review, final export, and YouTube packaging. A/V script approval has separate Director and Production reviews. | Resolve deadlines to deliverable or decision. Walk the parent, children, current version and affected recipients before moving dates. | **Editorial craft and distributed release — Early Learning Centre.** The campaign records show wording parity between signage and web, Service Design/SME review before web publication, a later embedded video, and a social package with its own green light. Another has program-specific web children, audience-specific copy and a delayed poster approval question. | Confirm series-level promise versus event detail, speaker/venue facts, partner recognition and round of partner review. | **Educator web content, service experience and reusable design — resource packages.** The team staged one page before scaling, checked text on responsive layouts, bundled classroom printables for a single download, and explored tracking on outgoing video links. | Have a subject expert check the educational purpose, design one complete example, and use the destination path from the teacher's perspective. ## Recurring execution and learning

1. At intake, capture the requested output, the user of the outcome, the actual unresolved constraint, authority and date interpretation. Store prior patterns as candidates with conditions.
2. At handoff, connect `current artifact version → authorized scope → receiving actor → destination → access check → receipt`. Keep the same downstream edges for parallel tasks and explicitly link further dependencies.
3. At revision, show the effect by output and successor. Retest changed notes against the active cut, identify earlier recipients needing the updated copy, and invalidate only the claims affected by the change.
4. At closeout, record planned time, observed active effort, time waiting for review, resends/rework, actual delivery state, audience evidence, and source quality as separate observations.
5. After multiple cases, promote a pattern into a reusable practice with triggers, boundary conditions, a counterexample and a receiving check. Examples: copy deadline controls when the first externally exposed upload will be sent; extra B-roll works only when it makes the viewer's task clearer; stage one educator journey before replicating the template. No observed outcome here requires creating a new persona.
