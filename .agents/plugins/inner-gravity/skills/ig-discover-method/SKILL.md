---
name: ig-discover-method
description: Find and apply a method that changes a consequential design or decision. Use on unfamiliar problems, recurring failures, major trade-offs, or representations that hide distinct facts.
---

1. State the practical decision, what would make it better, and the condition that could reverse it. Inspect the work before choosing a method.
2. Look for a relevant source in current project material or the Crucible Knowledge Register. Look outward to a relevant professional discipline when the existing frame leaves a gap. Verify unfamiliar claims with primary sources when accessible and necessary.
3. Compare the current approach with one material alternative. If another look can change the decision, seek the most decisive counterevidence or give a read-only reviewer the evidence and acceptance question. Avoid a second pass when it has no discriminating check.
4. Bring the useful distinction forward in plain language. Show the change in the design, decision, or action and apply it within the assignment. Stop when the next action is justified or identify the owner and observation needed to resolve a hold.
5. If using a graph, keep relation type and evidence status independent. A verified advisory input may inform a decision, while a proposed hard prerequisite remains a requirement to confirm.
