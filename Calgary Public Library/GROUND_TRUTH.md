- **Prior Plan (`2023–2026`):** *Everyone Belongs at the Library* (*Create Purpose Together*, *Champion a Sense of Belonging*, *Energize Lifelong Learning*).
- **Current Plan (`2027–2030`):** *A Better Tomorrow Through Stories* (public launch `2026-10-27`; post-launch transition in effect; final Board approval date `unknown`):
  - **Official Mandates (5):** **`Collections`**, **`Experiences`**, **`Expertise`**, **`Spaces`**, **`Technology`**.
  - **Values (3):** *Curiosity*, *Collaboration*, *Equity and Belonging*.
  - **Commitments (3):** *Intellectual Freedom*, *Truth and Reconciliation*, *Intentional Innovation*.

- **Internal Editorial Lenses (Distinct From Official Mandates):** `Grow` (practical capability), `Books & Reading` (reading discovery/collections), `Community` (people, spaces, belonging). Never present internal lenses as official 2027–2030 mandate names.

## 2. Authority Boundaries & Proposed Operating Gates `[PROPOSED WORKING MODEL]`

> **Qualification Note:** The `G01`–`G06` gates and role labels below reflect sampled Asana task patterns and comparative role research—**not binding Calgary Public Library policy**. Confirm actual named delegates and account access per project.

- **Public Leadership & Shared Stewardship:** Communications, Marketing, and Production sit under Acting Director, Communications (snapshot `2026-11`). YouTube channel stewardship is shared with another specialist (`sole_channel_authority: unknown`).
- **Role Boundaries (`Skills and Workflows/README.md` + `cpl_handoff_portable.yaml`):**
  - **Media Production (`PROD-D` / `PROD-M` / `PROD-S`):** Creative feasibility, A/V scripts, capture, edit, technical QC (`MP-10`), and caption sidecars.
  - **Marketing (`MKT-D` / `MKT-M` / `MKT-S`):** Portfolio tradeoffs, audience/channel strategy, campaign calendar placement, and wider social scheduling (Digital Marketing owns live social scheduling).
  - **Communications (`COM-D` / `COM-M` / `COM-S`):** Institutional posture, sensitive claims, public messaging clearance.
  - **Content Management (`CNT-D` / `CNT-M` / `CNT-S`):** Channel taxonomy, CMS/web pages (`calgarylibrary.ca`), metadata, asset versions, and currency maintenance (`CH-05`).
  - **Service / Programme Owners:** Factual confirmation (`CD-01`), programme eligibility, service capacity, and cultural/community permissions (`HOLD-04`).
- **Operating Gates (`G01`–`G06`) & `CHG-01` Change Rule:**
  - **`G01` Scope:** Named outcome, audience, use, and deliverable at intake/scope change.
  - **`G02` Ready for Production:** Authoritative brief + confirmed people, facts (`CD-01`), assets, and access/bookings.
  - **`G03` Production Review (`MP-10`):** Named review on an exact cut version, with conditions/holds.
  - **`G04` Stakeholder Review:** Scoped institutional/programme response and any remaining hold.
  - **`G05` Receiving Handoff (`CH-04`):** Sender + named receiving colleague confirm the exact version opens and supports the intended action without reconstruction.
  - **`G06` Release Authorization:** Explicit confirmation for exact asset, destination, and conditions (**default if unrecorded = `UNKNOWN — DO NOT RELEASE`**).
  - **`CHG-01` Selective Invalidation:** A cut revision (`v1 -> v2`, e.g. a timing trim) marks timing-dependent captions and cutdowns `STALE` and blocks `v2` acceptance/release on `v1` approval, while keeping unchanged assets (`cleared_still`, independent web copy) `VALID`.
- **Never-Infer Rules (`cpl_handoff_portable.yaml`):** Never infer approval from task completion, delivery from export, publication from unlisted upload, adoption from review, or individual impact from institutional metrics. Keep Internal Handoff, First External Access (including unlisted newsletter links), and Public Rollout distinct (`CH-04`).
