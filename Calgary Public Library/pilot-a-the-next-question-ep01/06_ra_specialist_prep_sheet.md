# Pilot A · Episode 1 Prep Sheet for Readers' Advisory Specialist

> **Series:** *The Next Question* — Episode 1 (*"One Storm, Under 220 Pages"*)  
> **What This Is:** A quick 1-page prep sheet so you know exactly what we need before shoot day — no memorization and no heavy production setup.  
> **Total On-Camera Time Needed:** ~90 minutes inside one quiet branch meeting room (`09:30–11:05`, including mic test and wrap stills).

---

## 1. The Episode Concept (~4.5 Minutes Total)
- **Viewer Dilemma:** *"I want a page-turner I can finish in one snowy weekend — no 600-page fantasy trilogies."*
- **Format:** Just you seated across a bare wooden table with three slim books and plain index cards. No podium, no stiff script reading — conversational, crisp, and opinionated.
- **Crew Footprint:** Just **two people** in the room (you on camera + one Producer/Camera/Sound operator).

### The 6 Quick Beats (No Teleprompter Required)
1. **The Hook:** Slide a thick 600-page "doorstopper" off the table and replace it with three slim paperbacks (`under 220 pages`).
2. **The Rejected Pick *(also framed for a short vertical clip, ~30–45s)*:** Set aside an obvious bestseller face-down and explain why three quick questions beat algorithm lists.
3. **Question 1 -> Pick 1 (Fast Momentum):** *"Pacing or atmosphere?"* Open Pick 1 flat to page 1 to show short, fast-moving lines — zero slow prologue.
4. **Question 2 -> Pick 2 (High Stakes, Small Room):** *"Hate epic quests?"* Stick a yellow tab on Pick 2 — tight tension in one room, zero filler.
5. **Question 3 -> Pick 3 (Non-Obvious Curveball):** *"Think you only read nonfiction?"* Present a gripping true story shorter than a commute playlist, and tell viewers they can look up all three in the Calgary Public Library catalogue to check live availability or place a hold.
6. **The Closer:** Flip the `"One storm. Under 220 pages"` card over, slide a blank card and pen forward, and invite viewers to drop their own weird one-line reading constraint in the comments for Episode 2.
