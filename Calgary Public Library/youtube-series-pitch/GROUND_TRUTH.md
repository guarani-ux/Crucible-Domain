## 3. Autonomous Working Defaults `[PROPOSED DEFAULT]`

To avoid stalling on unverified logistics while testing the full Writers' Room process end-to-end, the following reversible defaults govern this package:

1. **Target Pitch Room:** Communications & Engagement leadership + Media Production lead, with Digital Marketing coordination aligned on repurposing handoffs.
2. **Primary Pitch Decision Ask (`MW-03`):** Approve the 5-series portfolio framing as a repeatable editorial architecture and greenlight **1–2 bounded pilots** (`CH-02`: recommending `S1: The Next Question` + `S2: You Can Do That Here?`, with a lighter 1-pilot fallback).
3. **Production Ceiling:** Lean **1–2 person in-house crew** targeting **1–2 flagship YouTube episodes per month** plus modular Shorts/social assets captured inside the same shoot window (zero dedicated second camera unit or full-time external agency dependency assumed).
4. **Slot Allocation:** Upgrade the 2 existing local seeds (`S1` = `PITCH-NQ`, `S2` = `PITCH-YCD`) and originate 3 sharply contrasting series (`S3`–`S5`) so every slot holds a distinct Viewer Relationship Posture and Episode Engine.
5. **Platform Scope:** YouTube (Long-form + Shorts), Instagram (Reels/Carousels), Facebook, LinkedIn, `calgarylibrary.ca` destination pages, and segmented Email/Newsletters—with explicit **SKIP** rules where a series does not belong on a platform.
