### Series 1 · *The Next Question* *(Upgraded `PITCH-NQ`)*

- **10-Second Promise:** Give readers stuck in an algorithmic rut a fast, sharp 3-question tasting diagnostic that lands them on unexpected books/media they can look up to check live availability or place a hold.
- **Why a Series (Not a One-Off):** Taste is infinite. Once viewers learn the 3-question game, they return both to play along and to submit their own hyper-specific constraints in the comments for future episodes.
- **Context Anchor & Strategic Fit (`MK-02`):**
  - *Official Mandates (`2027–2030 Strategic Plan`):* **Collections**, **Experiences** *(Internal editorial lens: `Books & Reading`)*.
  - *Fit Sentence `[PROPOSED INTERNAL RATIONALE — Confirm with sponsor before any external use]`*: *"Helping stuck or curious Calgarians narrow down what to read, watch, or listen to next advances the 2027–2030 **Collections** and **Experiences** mandates because it connects patrons to catalogue depth through human reader advisory rather than generic algorithms."*

#### Modulation Profile (`02_modulation_board.md`)
- **Dial 1 · Viewer Relationship Posture:** **Trusted Tastemaker / Sparring Partner** — crisp, opinionated, empathetic, never solemn or passive.
- **Dial 2 · Narrative Engine (`Because -> But -> Therefore`) & Episode Variable:**
  - *Engine:* Viewer constraint arrives (*"I loved X, but I'm tired of Y"*) -> Staff specialist rejects the obvious algorithm pick (`But`) using 3 diagnostic questions -> Lands on 2–3 surprising, tactile picks (`Therefore`).
  - *Episode Variable:* One hyper-specific viewer constraint per episode.
- **Dial 3 · Camera & Sound Signature:**
  - `35mm` seated table axis (viewer sits across the table, not behind a podium).
  - **Visual-First Subtraction:** Never read book jackets aloud when the cover and a sticky-tabbed page spread are in frame (`5-shot coverage`: hands opening book, eyes reacting, tight spine stack, table wide, OTS question card).
  - **Nat Sound:** Crisp hardcover thump, page-riffle natural-sound pocket before the counter-pick lands.

  - *Engine:* Viewer tries a library task (`Because`) -> Hits the confusing button/eligibility trap (`But`) -> Specialist executes 3 exact moves (`Therefore`) that unlock it cleanly.
  - *Episode Variable:* One recurring patron or educator friction task.
- **Dial 3 · Camera & Sound Signature:**
  - Tight overhead hands + crisp high-legibility screen capture (`100%` readable at phone scale; zero microscopic cursor hunts).
  - **Mouth-Shape Syntax:** Right-branching imperatives (*"Tap Availability right here—not Place Hold"*), `1.5s` voice-off screen pauses (`[NAT SOUND POCKET]`) after every tap so viewers follow live on a second device.
