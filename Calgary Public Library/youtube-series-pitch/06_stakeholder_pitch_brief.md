# Calgary Public Library YouTube — 2027 Five-Series Pitch Brief

> **Audience:** Communications & Engagement Leadership, Media Production, Digital Marketing, and Content Strategy  
> **Read Time:** ~2 minutes  
> **Purpose:** Confirm our repeatable 5-series YouTube architecture and approve a small, low-risk pilot (1 or 2 episodes) before committing full 2027 calendar space.

---

## 1. Why This Proposal
Our Phase 1 audit of `213` Calgary Public Library videos (`2021–2026`) surfaced one core lesson: **getting discovered and keeping viewers watching are two different problems, and views alone do not tell us if a video worked.**

Right now, one-off promotional videos force us to start from zero every time. Instead of producing five versions of the same "Library explainer," this proposal organizes our YouTube channel into **five distinct series** — each solving a different patron question, tied to the *2027–2030 Strategic Plan* (**Collections, Experiences, Expertise, Spaces, Technology**), and built to run inside a lean **1–2 person in-house crew** at a realistic pace of **1–2 flagship episodes per month** across the entire channel (never all five at once).

*First check before shooting anything:* search the existing `213`-video archive so we reuse or update strong existing pieces rather than duplicating them.

---

## 2. The Five-Series Slate at a Glance

| Series | Patron Need It Solves | Strategic Plan Fit (`2027–2030`) | Estimated Crew Effort (All-In) | Recommended Timing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. The Next Question** | Stuck in a reading slump? A specialist uses 3 quick questions to cut past algorithm picks and land on 3 surprising books to check availability or place a hold. | Collections, Experiences | Low (~10–13 hrs/ep) | **Pilot A (Start Here)** |
| **2. You Can Do That Here?** | Everyday creative or tech hurdle solved live using free Library spaces, equipment, and staff expertise. | Technology, Spaces, Expertise | Medium (~15–20 hrs/ep) | **Pilot B** *(once room/gear access is confirmed)* |
| **3. Rooms That Make Things** | Calm, tactile, low-talk look inside makerspaces, crafts, and shared Library rooms for viewers craving real craft and belonging. | Spaces, Experiences | Low–Med (~11–15 hrs/ep) | Wave 2 *(after pilot review)* |
| **4. Ask a Specialist in 180 Seconds** | Fast, zero-fluff 3-minute walkthroughs fixing high-friction digital or catalogue roadblocks (embeddable directly on `calgarylibrary.ca`). | Expertise, Technology | Very Low *(batch 2–3 at once)* | Alternate Pilot / Web Utility |
| **5. Inside the Stack** | Backstage look at how books, sorting, conservation, and branch logistics actually work across the system. | Collections, Spaces, Expertise | Med–High (~16–22 hrs/ep) | Wave 2 *(requires facility clearance)* |

**Cross-Platform Principle ("Capture Once, Adapt Only Where Useful"):**  
Each flagship episode is framed cleanly so **1–2 native vertical clips** (YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels) and **2 still frames** (for `calgarylibrary.ca` or email newsletters — never embedded video inside email) come out of the same shoot window. Platforms that do not fit a series (for example, LinkedIn for reader advisory) are skipped on purpose.

---

## 3. Recommended Decision
- **Recommended Option (2 Pilots):** Approve the 5-series framework and greenlight **Pilot A (*The Next Question* Ep. 1)** immediately, plus **Pilot B (*You Can Do That Here?* Ep. 1)** once studio/equipment access is verified.
- **Lighter Option (1 Pilot):** Greenlight **only Pilot A (*The Next Question* Ep. 1 + 2 vertical clips)** at a single branch table with public collection books — zero special room or equipment dependencies.
- **How We Judge Success:** After Episode 1, review total crew hours (staying under `13 hrs` for Pilot A), 30-second hold and average watch duration against our 2026 baseline, viewer reading-constraint comments, and clicks to the curated catalogue list — then decide whether to continue, adjust, or pause.
