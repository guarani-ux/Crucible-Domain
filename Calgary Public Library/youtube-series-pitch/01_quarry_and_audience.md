## 1. The Diagnosis: Why Institutional YouTube Channels Stall (`213`-Video Audit Lens)

From the Media Content Framework Phase 1 team audit (`213` videos, `2021-01` to `2026-06` — `[VERIFIED LOCAL]`):
> *"Discoverability and retention are different problems; evaluate media beyond counts and views."*

Applying `MK-04` (`discover -> choose -> understand -> act`) yields **4 working editorial hypotheses** to test against the `2026` analytics baseline (`[HYPOTHESIS — VERIFY AGAINST HOLD-02; individual 213-video openings/retention curves not directly inspected per K-ARCH-03]`):

1. **Discovery Breaks When Content Leads With the Institution Instead of the Friction (`[HYPOTHESIS — CHECK HOLD-02]`):**
   - **Failure Pattern:** Opening on logos, branch exteriors, or strategic-plan phrasing (*"At Calgary Public Library, we advance Experiences..."*) before showing the human dilemma or physical proof.
   - **Audience Reality:** A tired parent, job-seeker, reader in a slump, or maker doesn't wake up wanting "institutional alignment"—they have a concrete moment of friction, curiosity, or loneliness.
2. **Retention Breaks When There Is No `Because -> But -> Therefore` Spine (`av-craft` / `[HYPOTHESIS — CHECK HOLD-02]`):**
   - **Failure Pattern:** "Topic buckets" (*"And also you can borrow..."*) or static talking-head tours that flatten tension into a verbal brochure.
   - **Audience Reality:** Viewers stay when the opening visual proves stakes in 5 seconds, hits a real turn/complication, and resolves into an actionable payoff.
3. **Habit Breaks When Videos Are One-Offs Without a Repeatable Posture (`[HYPOTHESIS — CHECK HOLD-02]`):**
   - **Failure Pattern:** Publishing isolated event promos or seasonal ads with no consistent host relationship, recurring viewer question, or two-way comment loop.
   - **Audience Reality:** A viewer subscribes to a **recognized promise and relationship**—knowing *what kind of human experience* waiting in the next episode will give them.
4. **Action Breaks at the Destination (`audience-and-campaigns.md#L27` & `institutional-evidence.md` `[VERIFIED LOCAL]`):**
   - Observed Calgary Public Library cases (*Educators hub vs. Stop Motion* grade mismatch in `audience-and-campaigns.md#L27`; *LitCon* split handoffs; *Ultimate Summer Challenge* evergreen vs. seasonal tension in `institutional-evidence.md`) prove that high clicks/views waste effort if the linked page is ambiguous, registration-gated without warning, or stale.

## 3. Producible Physical & Human Quarry (`av-craft`)

Instead of relying on heavy sets, scripted actors, or external agencies, this quarry inventories assets natively accessible inside a library system *(all live filming locations and staff appearances remain subject to `[HOLD-04]` clearances)*:

- **Acoustic & Tactile Room Texture (`NAT SOUND`):** Book drops and conveyor belts, automated sorter hum, page turns, quiet branch ambience, 3D-printer/sewing/audio-booth room tone, crisp tabletop object handling, muted footsteps in large civic architecture.
- **Physical Proof Objects (5-Second Visual Hooks):** A sticky note inside a returned novel, a microphone cable plugged into an interface, a freshly printed 3D/craft prototype on a wooden table, a physical library card scanning on a reader, an oversized branch transit bin rolling onto a dock.
- **Human Assets (Pending Consent & Schedule):** Front-line reader-advisory specialists, Tech Mentors / digital navigators, makerspace/studio coordinators, back-of-house logistics/collections staff, and willing patrons/educators *(zero filmed patrons or staff assumed without signed consent and service-owner approval)*.

### Quarry Synthesis Check (`MK-01`)
1. **Can we answer all 5 viewer tensions without inventing fake access?** Yes—every series starts with physical books, public spaces, digital/service routes, or staff expertise (`[HOLD-04]` gates exact branch/staff locks before shoot).
2. **Where is the single biggest differentiator vs. typical library YouTube?** **Deliberate Experience Modulation:** instead of running all five slots in the same explanatory "library presenter" tone, Stage 2 deliberately assigns five distinct *Viewer Relationship Postures* and *Camera/Sound Grammars* to match each tension.
