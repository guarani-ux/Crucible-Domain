## 2. Autonomous Local-First Intake & Pre-Finish Audit (`Push Right`)
1. **Synthesize Before Asking:** Resolve the problem, recipient, intended use, next action, sources, and task-specific success criteria from context; record them in the existing assignment. Check whether the proposed approach serves that result; adapt within the user's purpose, constraints, and authority. Gather local ground truth first, then investigate gaps using accessible authorized evidence. Ask where the owner's knowledge, taste, priorities, or authority changes the outcome. Tag reversible assumptions `[PROPOSED DEFAULT]`; reserve `[UNKNOWN — OWNER TO CONFIRM]` for gaps requiring owner input. Complete authorized independent work; hold only actions whose correctness or authority depends on unresolved information. Labels supply neither evidence nor permission.
2. **Mandatory Pre-Finish Audit:** Before consequential handoff, run read-only `ig-evidence-review` on the exact candidate, sources, revision, and authority. In the existing work record, identify each required review, reviewer, revision, findings, and status: `PASS` only after completed review with no blocking findings; `BLOCKED` for unresolved blockers; `UNAVAILABLE` when review cannot complete. Never substitute author self-review or claim an unavailable review passed. For holds, name the recovery action and owner; hold only dependent handoff. Resolve blockers and repeat affected checks; material changes invalidate affected reviews.

## 3. Work Operating System (`P-WOS`) & Boundary Rules
1. **Seven Graph Types:** Classify new graph views as capability inventory, reference process model, planning decision flow, plan dependency graph, execution and provenance trace, outcome map, or target system architecture.
2. **Stable Record IDs (`K-SIDECAR-01`):** Use shared IDs (`P-*`, `K-*`, `A01..A10`, `MW/MP/MK/CD/CH/OP-*`) across registers, prose, and Mermaid nodes.
3. **Blocking vs. Advisory Edges (`K-GRAPH-01`):** Use solid arrows (`-->`) for blocking dependencies and dashed arrows (`-.->`) for advisory links. Label evidence status separately (`verified` / `unverified`); an unverified blocker remains blocking.
4. **Planned vs. Observed Separation (`K-WOS-03`, `K-ARCH-03`):** Bind claims to the exact session, revision, or receipt. Distinguish instruction changes from observed runtime enforcement and recipient use. Record observed outcomes and reusable corrections with their conditions in existing records.
5. **External Source Boundaries:** Keep Asana strictly read-only. Keep restricted employer material in its authorized source system.

## 4. Orchestrator-Producer Role & Hub-and-Spoke MCP Rules (`K-MCP-01`)
1. **Single Control Plane (No Double Bureaucracy):** Crucible owns framing, authority resolution, coordination, and recipient usability checks. The commissioned domain workflow owns research, substantive development, writing, domain review, and revision within the assignment. Its agent applies the domain knowledge and craft the decisions require, including expertise the request does not name; verify access to necessary tools and sources before relying on them. Use the spoke's **deterministic domain mechanics** (form recognition, templates, timing/cut math, `.md`/`.docx` export) to support that work. Keep lightweight 3-file workspace plugins (`.agents/plugins/<spoke>/{plugin.json,mcp_config.json,skills/<spoke>/SKILL.md}`) and one coordinating control plane.
2. **Selection != Craft:** Never pipe raw `crucible_gate.py route` lexical rankings into generation packets. Recognize the deliverable form first; attach at most **one** form-pinned Crucible procedure (`MW-01`, `MW-02`, `MW-04`), abstaining (`null`) when none is pinned. Procedure selection does not select review requirements.
3. **Authority != Craft:** Crucible procedures enter spoke packets strictly as non-evidentiary craft guidance (`role: "method"`, `use: "craft"`). They never grant project facts, asset clearance, or cut approval; fresh or resumed cuts on existing material require project-bound decision authority or hold the affected action at preflight.
4. **Verification by Intended Use:** Derive review from the recipient, intended use, next action, and consequences of error; form and spoke defaults cannot lower these requirements. Exploratory work needs source/authority boundaries, clear proposals, and recipient usability; proposed fonts or lighting need no factual provenance. Work directing or authorizing execution needs applicable claim evidence, authority, feasibility, and timing checks, even in a `treatment` or `production_brief`. Public factual claims require claim-by-claim evidence regardless of form. Every re-edit handoff also requires independent native editorial review of the actual candidate against source material/current sequence: identifiable assets and versions, executable change instructions, applicable source/time references, timing/continuity, and caption/derivative impacts. Evidence audit cannot substitute. If required material or editorial capability is unavailable, apply §2.2. Declare readiness only for the reviewed use and revision. Inspect the actual delivered output through the available receiving surface for content, format, and fitness for the next action; distinguish observed usability from untested recipient steps. Finish when the need is met, or report the precise obstacle after investigating accessible remedies.
